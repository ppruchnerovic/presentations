# It's Not Vibe Coding If You Know What You're Doing

**Jeff Blankenburg (Principal Developer Advocate — Dynatrace)**

`Track: Developer Experience` · `Type: Keynote/Talk` · `Stage: Airstream 2`

## Abstract

We've all heard stories about how people are using AI to create entire software projects, and in every case, I had nothing but skepticism and doubt.

Until I tried it.

Let me show you how I build collectyourcards.com, from the ground up, in less than two weeks, without writing a single line of code.  We will talk about effective prompting, toolsets, and the do's and don'ts of coding with AI.

## Speakers

### Jeff Blankenburg

*Principal Developer Advocate — Dynatrace*

Jeff Blankenburg spent the early part of his career in digital advertising, building websites for Victoria’s Secret, Abercrombie & Fitch, and Ford Motor Company, among others. For the next 7 years, he worked on the Amazon Alexa team helping developers make Alexa even smarter. Today, he is the Head of Developer Relations for DigitalOcean. Jeff has also spoken at conferences all over the world, including London, Munich, Sydney, Tokyo, and New York, covering topics ranging from software development technologies to soft skill techniques.

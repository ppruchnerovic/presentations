# The Last Mile of AI: From Prototype to Production

**Clemens Wasner (CEO — enliteAI), Holger Hammel (VP of Engineering — HelloFresh), Martin Remmelgas (CEO — Codemagic), Max Jacobson (VP of Product — Antithesis), Marius Cosareanu (VP Software Engineering — UiPath)**

`Track: Strategy & Innovation` · `Type: Panel/Fireside Chat` · `Stage: Tech Leaders Stage`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-9vwGZ02aio) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/851)

## Abstract

Most AI looks impressive in a demo and never survives contact with production. This panel focuses on that last mile and what the teams who get to dependable, running systems do differently. Practitioners on what actually breaks, and how they fix it.

## Speakers

### Clemens Wasner

*CEO — enliteAI*

Clemens Wasner is CEO of enliteAI, an Austrian venture studio that achieved its first AI exit just 18 months after investment. He is also founder and chairperson of AI Austria, an independent think tank driving Austria’s competitiveness in artificial intelligence. Since 2017 he has published the Austrian AI Landscape, the reference overview of the country’s AI ecosystem. Clemens hosts the weekly “AI Talk” podcast and is an active member of the European AI Forum, a coalition of national AI associations that advises on and shapes EU regulation.

### Holger Hammel

*VP of Engineering — HelloFresh*

Holger Hammel is Vice President Engineering at HelloFresh, where he leads the Consumer Alliance – overseeing all customer-facing digital products and related services across HelloFresh brands globally. He joined the company in 2024 to further scale and strengthen its consumer technology ecosystem. With more than 15 years of leadership experience at companies such as eBay, Mobile International, Aiven, and OneFootball, Holger has a strong track record in building high-performing engineering organizations. He is particularly known for bridging the gap between engineering and business strategy, driving innovation, and delivering scalable, customer-centric solutions.

### Martin Remmelgas

*CEO — Codemagic*

Martin is CEO of Codemagic. Codemagic started as a CI/CD product for mobile developers and now offers and over-the-air (OTA) updates as well. Fun fact: Martin studied to become a chemist, but after graduation, he began his career in technology in 2017.

### Max Jacobson

*VP of Product — Antithesis*

Max began his career at Bridgewater Associates before moving into startups, including co-founding a company in a second language. He’s built and scaled teams across marketing, operations, finance, engineering, and product, bringing a systems-level perspective to building complex organizations and tools. Outside of work, Max loves traveling, cooking, and extremely long walks through cities and nature.

### Marius Cosareanu

*VP Software Engineering — UiPath*

has spent a decade at UiPath rewiring how the platform gets built, starting as a Software Engineer in 2016 and working his way up to VP, Software Engineering today. Along the way he became one of the people building the tools other UiPath engineers rely on: he maintains the UiPath Python SDK, the LangChain SDK for LangGraph agents, and the MCP SDK for running local servers on the platform, the kind of infrastructure work that holds up the UiPath AI-agent ambitions. Ten years in, he's gone from writing code to writing the code other engineers depend on, proof that at UiPath, staying technical and moving up aren't mutually exclusive.

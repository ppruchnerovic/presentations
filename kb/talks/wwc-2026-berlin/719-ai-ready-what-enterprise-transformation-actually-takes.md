# AI-Ready? What Enterprise Transformation Actually Takes

**Florian Deter (General Manager EMEA Strategic Partnerships — Microsoft), Mei Dent (Chief Product and Technology Officer — TeamViewer), Oliver Krizek (CEO and Founder — NAVAX), Stefan Lemke (CIO — SIGNAL IDUNA Group), Uli Irnich (CIO Advisor EMEA — Salesforce)**

`Track: Strategy & Innovation` · `Type: Panel/Fireside Chat` · `Stage: Tech Leaders Stage`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=16Gf5OwAKf8) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/719)

## Abstract

Every enterprise is running AI pilots. Very few are actually transformed. The gap between a successful proof of concept and an organization that has genuinely changed how it operates is where most companies get stuck - and it has almost nothing to do with the technology. The real barriers are data governance, change management and business process redesign. This panel brings together leaders who have lived both sides of that gap - those building the transformation from the inside, those enabling it from the outside, and those who have seen enough enterprise AI journeys to know exactly where and why they fail. The conversation will cut through the noise of announcements and demos to address what enterprise AI transformation actually requires: organizational courage, the right infrastructure decisions, and a clear-eyed understanding that this is also very much a human challenge, not only a technical one.

## Speakers

### Florian Deter

*General Manager EMEA Strategic Partnerships — Microsoft*

Florian Deter currently serves as General Manager EMEA Strategic Partner Sales, He has been responsible before for Microsoft's enterprise business in Germany as Managing Director of Microsoft Germany and Enterprise Commercial Lead since August 1, 2022.

Florian began his career in consulting and management roles at Oliver Wyman, Accenture, and PwC. He later served as Executive Vice President overseeing the global portfolio of Nordcloud, an IBM company. Prior to that, he led CIO Advisory and the Cloud Applications Service Line at IBM.

### Mei Dent

*Chief Product and Technology Officer — TeamViewer*

Mei Dent has an excellent track record as a technology executive in the software and IT industry. She started her career as a software developer at IBM and spent nearly 25 years in various product and engineering related leadership roles at Canadian software giant OpenText. As part of the senior leadership team of the company’s software development organization, she was responsible for global engineering practices and processes for security, performance, user experience, as well as reusable and shared services. In her most recent role as CTO at customer communications management and engagement software provider Doxim, Mei led the entire technology organization and scaled it to support the company’s growth.

Mei holds a Master in Computer Science from the Western University in Ontario.

### Oliver Krizek

*CEO and Founder — NAVAX*

Oliver Krizek is the Founder and CEO of the NAVAX Group. Mid-sized and large enterprises rely on the company’s expertise in digital business processes and automation.

With his vision and strong focus on innovation, Krizek has grown NAVAX from its entrepreneurial beginnings in the 1990s into an international group with more than 330
employees. He combines technical know-how and entrepreneurial experience with clear, decisive leadership.

Off the business stage, the father of three is an enthusiastic skier, golfer, and triathlete. His energy and positive attitude towards life shape not only his athletic ambitions but also the future of NAVAX.

### Stefan Lemke

*CIO — SIGNAL IDUNA Group*

Stefan Lemke began his career as an IT architect, consultant, and manager of transformation projects at Accenture after completing his studies in physics and his doctorate, ultimately serving as a Partner in Germany and the Netherlands. From 2010, he held leadership positions at Allianz Technology in Munich and Paris before moving to the Zurich Group in Germany as CIO. In 2017, he was appointed to the Board of Management of AXA Konzern AG, where he drove agile organization and culture, technological modernization, and the renewal of the application landscape.

Since 2023, Stefan Lemke has been a member of the Boards of Management of the SIGNAL IDUNA Group. He says: "Application development is a core business process, differentiating for our customers and distribution partners - and no longer conceivable without AI, cloud, and an agile setup."

### Uli Irnich

*CIO Advisor EMEA — Salesforce*

Uli Irnich is CIO Advisor EMEA at Salesforce, based in Düsseldorf. Drawing on over 14 years of experience as a CIO and Managing Director at major telecommunications companies — including Vodafone, Liberty Global, Telefonica, and E-Plus Group — Uli now strategically advises technology buyers and fellow CIOs within the expansive Salesforce ecosystem.

His professional background is centered on driving complex corporate initiatives and technological innovation. He is a recognized expert in Digital Transformation and IT Modernization, successfully leading transitions to agile business models and implementing large-scale initiatives like Tech2025.

Uli has managed six significant Merger and Acquisition activities, from planning through post-merger integration, notably utilizing MuleSoft for the Unitymedia-Vodafone integration. His focus on Operational Excellence includes Six Sigma expertise and Application Rationalization that delivers measurable financial synergies.

As a thought leader, Uli specializes in topics like the Agentic Enterprise and the future of the digital workforce. He actively shares insights through his popular podcast, "Digital Pacemaker," and is a frequent speaker at major industry events.

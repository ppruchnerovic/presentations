## Abstract

As software companies scale, engineering challenges change. The biggest bottleneck is no longer writing code — it is how teams use their time, attention, and decision-making capacity.

This session draws from Pipedrive’s transformation journey. Agur Jõgi, tech leader and former CTO of Pipedrive, shares behind-the-scenes lessons from scaling the company from a startup CRM into a global platform used by over 100,000 companies worldwide. He explores how the company evolved from a product-led growth mindset toward an engineering-led growth approach, where technology, scalability, and engineering excellence became strategic drivers of business growth.

Moving beyond an engineering-output mindset, Agur explores how Pipedrive redesigned its development model around AI, automation, and better human decision-making. He shares practical lessons from building an AI-native engineering organization — from reducing operational friction and automating repetitive work to improving organizational memory and enabling teams to focus more on customer and business impact.

## Speakers

### Agur Jõgi

*Tech leader and former CTO — Pipedrive*

Agur Jõgi is an experienced technology leader with extensive experience in building and scaling SaaS platforms. He most recently served as Chief Technology Officer at Pipedrive, a leading sales CRM platform used by over 100,000 companies globally. With his 35 years of experience across Skype, Bigbank, Elion, and askRobin, Agur shares insights into his leadership through engineering and product innovations. At Pipedrive, he has championed the adoption of data-driven product strategies, blending deep technical execution with a user-first mindset.

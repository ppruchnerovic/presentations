# The Limits of LLMs in Real-World Applications

**Deivids Vilkinsons (CEO — HASH), Guillaume Esnou (Field CTO Europe — Invisible Technologies), Laura Moritz (Manager, Data & AI — Globant), Mariam Hakobyan (CEO and Co-Founder — Softr)**

`Track: AI Engineering` · `Type: Panel/Fireside Chat` · `Stage: Tech Leaders Stage`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IlekqKuGBPA) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/992)

## Abstract

Plenty of organizations have an AI strategy on paper; far fewer have it genuinely working. This panel is about closing that gap, with leaders who have moved from ambition to results. A practical conversation about making AI real across a business.

## Speakers

### Deivids Vilkinsons

*CEO — HASH*

Dei is the CEO of HASH, the open-source platform for data integration and simulation. HASH develops ultra high-accuracy AI solutions for firms in safety-critical, error-sensitive, and regulated industries.

HASH has been developing "agentic" simulation tooling since 2019, when Dei established the firm with Joel Spolsky (the former founder of Stack Overflow, Trello, and Glitch). Today, HASH is profitable and has offices in New York, London, and Berlin.

Dei also leads one of the technical areas of the UK Advanced Research + Invention Agency's "Safeguarded AI" program, focused on developing high-trust interfaces for effective human-AI collaboration.

### Guillaume Esnou

*Field CTO Europe — Invisible Technologies*

Guillaume Esnou is Field CTO and Head of Technology for Invisible Technologies in EMEA, where he leads AI deployment strategy and technical delivery for enterprise clients. He brings deep expertise in AI and analytics, having built and scaled production-grade AI systems across sectors at McKinsey as an Associate Partner. Most recently, he led Engineering, Insights & Analytics at Surveyor Capital, Citadel hedge fund’s leading fundamental equities strategy. Guillaume holds an M.S. in Operations Research from Columbia University and a Diplôme d’Ingénieur in Mechanical and Industrial Engineering from Arts et Métiers. At Invisible, he focuses on building scalable AI systems that transform operations and drive measurable business impact.

### Laura Moritz

*Manager, Data & AI — Globant*

Laura Moritz is a Tech Manager for Data & AI at Globant, where she leads AI and data programs for enterprise clients across industries including automotive, healthcare, finance, and education. With 10 years of consulting experience, she works at the intersection of AI strategy, data products, and implementation — from LLM-powered solutions and predictive models to data platforms and analytics.
Beyond her corporate work, Laura is engaged with Women in Tech, supporting initiatives that bring more women in technology onto stages and into leadership.

### Mariam Hakobyan

*CEO and Co-Founder — Softr*

Mariam Hakobyan is the CEO and co-founder of Softr, the leading AI platform for building business apps without code. A former engineer turned entrepreneur, she has spent more than a decade building products and leading product and engineering teams, and is passionate about making software accessible to everyone.

# The Sustainability Race: AI's Promises, Pitfalls and Potential

**Alexander Gerfer (CTO — Würth Elektronik), Gülnaz Öneş (Group CIO — RWE), Wolfgang Oels (COO — Ecosia), Awi Lifshitz (CEO — weXelerate)**

`Track: Strategy & Innovation` · `Type: Panel/Fireside Chat` · `Stage: Tech Leaders Stage`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XCdkpr1HUxs) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/798)

## Abstract

AI and sustainability pull in two directions at once - a heavy demand on resources, and a powerful tool for tackling the problems that matter. Leaders from very different corners of industry come together to weigh where that balance really lands. An open conversation about what AI promises here, and what it actually delivers.

## Speakers

### Alexander Gerfer

*CTO — Würth Elektronik*

As Chief Technology Officer, Alexander Gerfer is responsible for establishing the Würth Elektronik eiSos Group as a technology enabler and service leader. The company is one of Europe's largest manufacturers of electronic and electromechanical components and part of the Würth Group. Gerfer began his career at Würth Elektronik eiSos 29 years ago as a technical sales representative. Since then, he has held various positions in the company.
In his role as CTO, he particularly promotes technology partnerships in future-oriented areas. With his expert knowledge, he passionately supports and advises innovative start-ups, especially when they encounter unexpected technical problems in the implementation of their ideas.
In line with the principle ‘Electronics for Positive Impact’, Alexander Gerfer is committed to sustainable, environmentally friendly and socially forward-looking technologies. For example, the company is researching LED light recipes for optimal photosynthesis and more efficient electronics. Würth Elektronik particularly supports innovative, ecological start-ups, for example in the areas of algae and mushroom cultivation, harvesting robots or biodiversity measurement.
During the course of his career, Gerfer has also played a key role in the development and expansion of research and development, as well as product, quality and supply chain management. As an author of specialist books, the electrical engineer and trained radio and television technician presents complex interrelations in an easy-to-understand and practical way. He is also a sought-after keynote speaker at conferences around the world.

### Gülnaz Öneş

*Group CIO — RWE*

As Group CIO at RWE, Gülnaz Öneş spearheads the energy transition through technology, with a strong emphasis on Data & AI and business agility.

She brings a distinguished track record across the automotive, financial services, and energy sectors. Gülnaz spent more than two decades within the Daimler
Group, holding executive roles including CTO at Daimler Truck and CIO at Mercedes-Benz Mobility.
Known for her people-first, integrative leadership style, Gülnaz turns technology and data into tangible business outcomes, strengthening collaboration and delivering measurable impact across global organisations.

### Wolfgang Oels

*COO — Ecosia*

Dr. Wolfgang Oels studied engineering and business and started his career at McKinsey & Company before taking senior leadership positions in the solar industry in Germany and the US. Since 2016, he has served as COO of Ecosia, the largest European search engine, and as Director of European Search Perspective, building a sovereign European web index.

### Awi Lifshitz

*CEO — weXelerate*

Awi Lifshitz is a passionate and visionary leader in the fields of innovation and digital transformation with more than 20 years of professional experience. His core competencies include innovation consulting, digitalization, and the development of new business strategies. As CEO of weXelerate, he leads Austria’s most significant innovation ecosystem, which provides space for innovators, fosters collaboration, and empowers individuals and organizations to unlock their full innovation potential. Driven by the mission to holistically support companies in innovation and transformation, Awi Lifshitz brings diverse perspectives and experiences gained across various industries, regions, and roles.

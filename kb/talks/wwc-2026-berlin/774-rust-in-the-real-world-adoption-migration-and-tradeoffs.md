## Abstract

An overview of how Rust is adopted in real engineering organizations, covering common adoption paths, migration strategies, and typical challenges teams face. The session examines the benefits Rust brings in production, the tradeoffs involved, and practical criteria for deciding where Rust is an appropriate choice and where it is not.

## Speakers

### Vitaly Bragilevsky

*Head of Rust Ecosystem — JetBrains*

Vitaly is the Head of Rust Ecosystem at JetBrains. He is passionate about programming languages (all of them!) and works closely with Rust, and other projects at JetBrains. As the author of "Haskell in Depth", he firmly believes that writing code in a functional style and avoiding mutation whenever possible leads to better outcomes.

# Surviving the Vulnpocalypse: Open Source and Supply Chain Security in a Post Mythos World

## Abstract

Software security has fundamentally changed. Advances in AI are accelerating vulnerability discovery, shrinking the time between disclosure and exploitation, and making sophisticated attacks accessible to a wider range of adversaries.

Organisations need to be taking this threat seriously. Don't panic, but do take action.

This talk will walk through the seriousness of the situation before focusing on remediations -- how to mythos-proof your code and systems. We'll look at running LLMs defensively as well as more traditional security methods to provide protection.

## Speakers

### Adrian Mouat

*Technical Community Advocate — Chainguard*

Adrian has been involved with containers from the early days of Docker and authored the O’Reilly book “Using Docker”.

He works at Chainguard  whose mission is to make the software lifecycle secure by default. His current focus is on improving the standard of security and provenance guarantees in container images.

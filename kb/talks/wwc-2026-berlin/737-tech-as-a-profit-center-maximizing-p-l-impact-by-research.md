# Tech as a Profit Center – Maximizing P&L Impact by Research Funding and Capitalization

**Henriette Brune (Chief of Staff to the CTO — FINN)**

## Abstract

Tech organizations are often seen as cost centers: valuable, but rarely measured in terms of direct P&L contribution. Yet there is enormous untapped potential: many startups and scale-ups miss out on research funding and capitalization opportunities that could directly improve cash flow and profitability.

In this session, Henriette Brune, Chief of Staff at FINN, shares how the company turned R&D from a cost driver into a profit lever by leveraging Germany’s "Forschungszulage" and capitalization strategies. Smaller companies in particular often underestimate the impact. Either because they don’t know these instruments exist or because they assume the overhead is too high. Henriette will demonstrate how FINN built a streamlined, largely automated process that keeps the effort for engineers and leaders minimal and non-disruptive, while still creating significant financial impact.

Attendees will learn how to design lightweight processes that connect engineering work to financial reporting, how to automate compliance using tools like Personio, JIRA, Make, and OpenAI, and how to align tech and finance stakeholders around shared goals.

The outcome: a playbook that allows tech leaders to keep their teams focused on innovation, while turning overlooked funding and capitalization opportunities into real contributions to the company’s bottom line.

## Speakers

### Henriette Brune

*Chief of Staff to the CTO — FINN*

Henriette Brune is Chief of Staff at FINN, one of Germany’s fastest-growing mobility startups. In her role, she works at the intersection of technology, business strategy, and organizational design, driving initiatives that accelerate innovation, scale tech effectiveness, and unlock hidden business value.

Her path into tech leadership wasn’t traditional: starting her career in HR, Henriette now partners closely with FINN’s CTO and engineering leadership. This cross-disciplinary background allows her to connect dots that are often siloed, linking engineering, product, and business departments to deliver measurable impact.

At FINN, she has led projects such as the capitalization of R&D efforts and designing innovation frameworks that helped the company move quickly on frontier technologies like Agentic AI. She is passionate about transforming tech organizations from “cost centers” into profit multipliers, and about building structures where ambitious goals, fast iteration, and even failure become catalysts for growth.

Henriette speaks on automation, innovation, and organizational scaling, sharing practical playbooks for leaders who want to combine speed with resilience in fast-moving industries.

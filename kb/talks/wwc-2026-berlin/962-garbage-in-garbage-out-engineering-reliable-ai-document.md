## Abstract

Your OCR problem isn't really an OCR problem. It’s everything that happens before and after it.

Most AI pipelines don’t fail because the model is bad. They fail because we send it garbage. We throw blurry mobile scans, crooked receipts, and massive blocks of unstructured text at an LLM and wonder why it’s expensive, inconsistent, and hallucinatory.



In this talk, you’ll see how to design an end-to-end document extraction pipeline that:

Validates and improves image quality at capture time

Extracts structured, high-context data instead of dumping raw text

Sends lean, intentional payloads to LLMs so they’re cheaper and more predictable

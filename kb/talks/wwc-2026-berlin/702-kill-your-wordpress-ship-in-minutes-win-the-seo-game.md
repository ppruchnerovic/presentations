## Abstract

We replaced a slow, dev-dependent WordPress/Elementor setup with a modern AI-native stack and didn't just ship faster. We won the SEO game too.

The stack: Lovable (AI page builder, locked to design system) → GitHub (version control + CI) → Cloudflare Pages + Workers (edge hosting) → Headless CMS (content teams self-serve, zero dev involvement).

In 5 minutes, you'll see how it works — two scenarios, one pipeline. We build a landing page with you and deploy it in minutes. A content editor publishes an article without touching a single line of code.

And the proof? We'll open Google Search Console live on stage.
Real outcomes: new pages ship in minutes, content teams are fully autonomous, hosting costs dropped, daily deployments became the norm, and organic traffic went through the roof.
Sounds too good to be true? It kind of is, and we are wondering why... Maybe you have the answer for us?

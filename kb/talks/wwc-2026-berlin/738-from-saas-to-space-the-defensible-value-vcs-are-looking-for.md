# From SaaS to Space: The Defensible Value VCs Are Looking For

**Frank Seehaus (Co-Founder and Managing Partner — Acton Capital), Friederike Hoffmann (Partner — b2venture), Nils Eiteneyer (Partner — Capnamic), Trevor Cox (Partner — i5invest)**

`Track: Strategy & Innovation` · `Type: Panel/Fireside Chat` · `Stage: Tech Leaders Stage`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wmomRB5AlaA) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/738)

## Abstract

Building unassailable moats. In a crowded market, brilliant code alone is no longer enough to survive. This panel serves as a strategic reality check on true technological defensibility, stretching the spectrum from highly efficient, metrics-driven SaaS to high-risk engineering frontiers like Space Tech to answer one core question: How do you build a business that can't be copied? We will explore whether defensibility now requires proprietary data loops, hardware integration, or deep infrastructure, while breaking down how VCs evaluate value across different horizons—balancing the quick traction metrics of software against the immense stamina required for Deep Tech—to reveal where the smart money is quietly moving next.

## Speakers

### Frank Seehaus

*Co-Founder and Managing Partner — Acton Capital*

Frank Seehaus is co-founder and managing partner at Acton Capital Partners. Based in Munich, Acton is a growth equity investor in tech enabled businesses across Europe and North America. Investment focus are companies with proven business models and a sustainable value proposition specifically in fintech, SaaS, marketplaces, healthtech and eCommerce. Previously, Frank was managing director at Burda Digital Ventures, the corporate VC of Hubert Burda Media Group, and Vice President at Deutsche Bank’s corporate finance division.

### Friederike Hoffmann

*Partner — b2venture*

Friederike Hoffmann is a Partner at b2venture, one of the leading early-stage venture capital firms in the German-speaking world. With a background in senior leadership at Swisscom, she brings deep operational experience in telecommunications and digital infrastructure to her investment work. At b2venture, Friederike focuses on Space, Defense and European technology sovereignty — areas she views as defining the continent's strategic future. Central to her work is mobilising private capital and activating experienced entrepreneurs as angel investors through b2venture's angel network, which she sees as a key lever for strengthening Europe's startup ecosystem. She serves on the Board of Directors of Ivoclar, a global leader in dental technology. Friederike holds a doctorate from the University of St. Gallen (HSG) and is based in Zurich.

### Nils Eiteneyer

*Partner — Capnamic*

Dr. Nils Eiteneyer is a Partner at Capnamic where they invest in exceptional early-stage tech founders. Before joining Capnamic, Nils co-founded xdeck, a boutique accelerator and pre-seed VC firm, and reorganized and scaled AEVOR & pinqponq, the sustainable lifestyle growth business at FOND OF Group, as an operator and co-managing director. Nils used to be a consultant at McKinsey & Company and holds a PhD in entrepreneurial finance and innovation from RWTH Aachen University. He studied business economics and social sciences at The London School of Economics and The University of Mannheim. As a former advisor and then operator, Nils very much tries to look at things from a builder's and founder's perspective

### Trevor Cox

*Partner — i5invest*

Trevor Cox has spent 28 years in mergers and acquisitions across Silicon Valley and Seattle. He began his career as an attorney at Gray Cary Ware & Freidenrich, one of the original Silicon Valley law firms. He went on to lead Strategy and Transformation at Brocade, a Fortune 1000 data center company, served as Managing Director of EMEA at Infor, and held a senior M&A role at Wyse, both private equity-backed technology companies. He later founded Eisbach Group, a corporate development advisory firm, and DataCloud, an AI company serving process manufacturing, which he led to a successful exit. He now leads sell-side M&A engagements at i5 Growth Partners in artificial intelligence, data infrastructure, agentic technology, enterprise software, and AgTech/FoodTech for lower middle market companies. Trevor holds a JD and MBA from UC Berkeley and Columbia Business School.

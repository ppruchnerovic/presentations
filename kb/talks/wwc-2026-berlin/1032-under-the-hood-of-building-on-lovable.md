# Under the Hood of Building on Lovable

**Fabian Hedin (Co-founder and CTO — Lovable)**

`Track: Strategy & Innovation` · `Type: Keynote/Talk` · `Stage: Mainstage`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=heVaZ1SpldA) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/1032)

## Abstract

Fabian Hedin co-founded Lovable to turn intent into working software. As CTO, he shares what happens under the hood of the platform millions build on — and shows how Lovable became the engine for building powerful software that your own tools and AI agents can operate and build with. A keynote about the next era of how software gets built, shipped, and run.

## Speakers

### Fabian Hedin

*Co-founder and CTO — Lovable*

Fabian Hedin is an accomplished entrepreneur and technology leader with a diverse background in founding and leading innovative companies—including serving as Co-Founder and CTO of Lovable, Founder and CEO of Tentium, and Founder and CTO of TenFAST. His earlier experience includes roles as Frontend Lead at Depict and Fullstack Engineer at CurbFood.

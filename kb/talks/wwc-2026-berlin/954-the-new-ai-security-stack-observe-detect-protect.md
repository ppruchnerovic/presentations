# The New AI Security Stack: Observe, Detect, Protect

**Jyoti Bansal (Founder and CEO — Harness), Christian Trummer (Co-founder and Chief Scientist — Bitpanda), Tomislav Tipurić (CTO — Nephos), Milin Desai (CEO — Sentry)**

`Track: Security & Privacy` · `Type: Panel/Fireside Chat` · `Stage: Mainstage`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DcGd21KYJNc) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/954)

## Abstract

As AI spreads through every system, the way we secure and observe those systems has to change with it. This panel brings together the people building that new stack to discuss what it means to stay in control. A look at how security and observability evolve in an AI-saturated world.

## Speakers

### Jyoti Bansal

*Founder and CEO — Harness*

Jyoti Bansal is a multi-unicorn founder and serial technology entrepreneur. He is the founder and CEO of Harness, the $5.5B AI Software Delivery Platform automating everything after code — testing, deployment, security, and compliance — for the largest enterprises in the world. Previously, he founded AppDynamics (acquired by Cisco for $3.7B), launched startup studio BIG Labs, and co-founded VC firm Unusual Ventures. He has been recognized as EY Entrepreneur of the Year, Forbes' Best Cloud Computing CEO, SiliconANGLE's Most Innovative Tech Startup Leader, and ranked No. 24 on Fortune's Most Innovative Companies list. He holds a BS in Computer Science from IIT Delhi and over 25 U.S. patents.

### Christian Trummer

*Co-founder and Chief Scientist — Bitpanda*

Christian Trummer is the founder and Chief Scientist of Bitpanda. Previously serving as CTO for nearly a decade, he scaled the engineering team to over 400 people, building the company's core trading platform and in-house banking system. He now leads a specialized team focused on embedding AI across the organization to enhance products and efficiency. Christian combines deep technical expertise with a vision for AI-driven transformation in fintech.

### Tomislav Tipurić

*CTO — Nephos*

Tomislav Tipurić is a business and technology professional who connects technical expertise with business strategy to clarify new technologies and drive corporate innovation. As a Chief Technology Officer and Lead Hackathon Coach, he focuses on artificial intelligence, multi-cloud platforms, and modern software development practices. He has designed more than 20 custom learning hackathons and delivered over 500 hands-on educational experiences, including more than 150 sessions dedicated to machine learning, generative AI, and agentic AI solutions. Tomislav translates technical concepts into business strategies, delivering workshops and lectures to C-level executives on the business impact of AI in their industries. Additionally, he has experience in sales enablement and corporate training, serving as a program trainer to help enterprise partners market, sell, and adopt generative AI and cloud productivity tools, and delivering cloud sales enablement programs for global telecommunications firms. Tomislav is certified and authorized as an official trainer by Google and Microsoft.

### Milin Desai

*CEO — Sentry*

Milin Desai currently serves as the CEO of Sentry. Used by more than 4 million developers and 150,000 organizations worldwide, Sentry helps every developer detect, understand, and fix broken code, fast. Milin joined Sentry from VMware where he helped build out multiple lines of business from networking (0->1b+) to multi cloud (0->100M+ SAAS). Earlier in his career, he held engineering and product roles at Veritas and Riverbed.

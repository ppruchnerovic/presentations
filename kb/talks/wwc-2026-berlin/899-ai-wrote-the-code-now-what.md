# AI Wrote the Code. Now What?

**Jyoti Bansal (Founder and CEO — Harness), Daniel Gebler (CTO — Picnic)**

`Track: —` · `Type: Panel/Fireside Chat` · `Stage: Stage 11`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8kNnlM21F_o) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/899)

## Abstract

Getting AI to generate code is the easy part. Getting that code shipped, safely and at speed, is where most teams are still stuck.

Jyoti Bansal, CEO and Co-founder of Harness, examines the growing gap between AI-assisted development and production-ready delivery, and the rise of autonomous agents closing it.

He'll share what the evolution of software delivery means for developers and engineering teams trying to make AI investment actually pay off.

## Speakers

### Jyoti Bansal

*Founder and CEO — Harness*

Jyoti Bansal is a multi-unicorn founder and serial technology entrepreneur. He is the founder and CEO of Harness, the $5.5B AI Software Delivery Platform automating everything after code — testing, deployment, security, and compliance — for the largest enterprises in the world. Previously, he founded AppDynamics (acquired by Cisco for $3.7B), launched startup studio BIG Labs, and co-founded VC firm Unusual Ventures. He has been recognized as EY Entrepreneur of the Year, Forbes' Best Cloud Computing CEO, SiliconANGLE's Most Innovative Tech Startup Leader, and ranked No. 24 on Fortune's Most Innovative Companies list. He holds a BS in Computer Science from IIT Delhi and over 25 U.S. patents.

### Daniel Gebler

*CTO — Picnic*

Daniel Gebler is CTO of Picnic, the world’s fastest growing online supermarket that makes grocery shopping simple, fun, and affordable for everyone.

Previously, he was Director R&D of Fredhopper, responsible for the product and technology roadmap, and led engineering teams located in Amsterdam and Sofia.

Daniel holds a PhD in Computer Science and an MBA. He published papers, edited journals, and chaired international workshops on software correctness.

# The Golden Age of Email: Owning the Inbox in the Age of AI

**Nikhil Gandhi (SVP of Engineering — Yahoo Mail), Dana Lawson (CTO — Netlify)**

`Track: AI Agents` · `Type: Panel/Fireside Chat` · `Stage: Stage 11`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uO00c-kVN2s) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/753)

## Abstract

Email has fundamentally stayed the same for the last 25 years but the rise of AI and agents are changing that. In this fireside chat, Nikhil Gandhi, Yahoo Mail’s  SVP of Engineering, will sit down to discuss Yahoo’s AI strategy for solving its “98% problem” — the reality that people ignore nearly all of their email yet still rely on it heavily to organize their lives– by shifting the inbox from a reactive tool to one that works for users. Join the discussion to learn about the delicate balance of maintaining decades of user trust while innovating quickly to integrate AI to reimagine the personal inbox.

## Speakers

### Nikhil Gandhi

*SVP of Engineering — Yahoo Mail*

Nikhil Gandhi is the Senior Vice President of Engineering for Yahoo Mail, where he oversees global engineering, product, and design for one of the world’s largest consumer communication platforms. He oversees a global team, leading technical strategy and execution across core messaging infrastructure, AI-driven experiences, spam and safety systems, and platform modernization, while driving user growth, engagement, and monetization. His organization also supports critical priorities including privacy, data governance, security, and global infrastructure at scale.

Prior to Yahoo, Nikhil was Head of Engineering for Ads Platform at TikTok, responsible for monetization systems powering a significant share of revenue, including creator tools and in-app commerce. He previously held leadership roles at Meta, scaling the Ads Experience organization globally, and spent a decade at Apple helping grow the Apple Online Store into a multi–tens of billions business while leading global engineering for Apple.com. A deeply technical leader, he combines strong product and platform instincts with a track record of building high-performing global teams and delivering complex systems at scale.

### Dana Lawson

*CTO — Netlify*

Dana Lawson is the Chief Technology Officer at Netlify, where she leads the Engineering, Product, and Design teams to innovate and deliver transformative digital experiences. With over two decades of leadership at industry-leading companies like GitHub, Heptio, and New Relic, Dana has a proven track record of scaling teams, fostering collaboration, and navigating complex technical landscapes.

Throughout her career, she’s worn many hats across the product lifecycle, showcasing her versatility and expertise building impactful technology solutions. Driven by a genuine passion for people, Dana infuses her leadership with a blend of technical expertise and a spirit of fun, creating an inspiring and dynamic work environment.

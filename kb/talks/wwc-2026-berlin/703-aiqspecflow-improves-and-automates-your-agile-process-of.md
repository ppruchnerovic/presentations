# AIQSpecFlow: Improves and automates your agile process of specification and creation of testcases.

**Rene Schenk (Software Architect — iQMatix)**

`Track: Quality & Reliability` · `Type: Startup Presentation` · `Stage: Airstream 1`

## Abstract

Good software starts with clear requirements. Discover how AIQSpecFlow helps you automatically create structured epics and user stories, right where others are still sorting and formulating theirs. AIQSpecFlow identifies gaps in test case coverage, assists with the creation of test cases in natural language, and enables the generation of executable test cases for various test frameworks. Higher quality and significantly less manual effort.

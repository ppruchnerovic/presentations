# Can This Elephant Dance? IBM Bob and the Future of AI-First Software Development

**Neel Sundaresan (General Manager AI Products/Productivity Tools — IBM), Arvid Ottenberg (Senior Product Manager IBM Bob at IBM — IBM)**

`Track: Strategy & Innovation` · `Type: Keynote/Talk` · `Stage: Mainstage`

## Abstract

In less than a decade, generative AI has completely reshaped software development. We are now entering, what I call,  the era of Software 4.0, where orchestrated agents are changing how software systems are conceived, built, and maintained. In this talk, I will share the origin and evolution of IBM Bob, our AI-first platform for enterprise-scale software development. I will show how Bob helps organizations apply AI across the full software lifecycle in real-world enterprise settings.

## Speakers

### Neel Sundaresan

*General Manager AI Products/Productivity Tools — IBM*

Neel Sundaresan is General Manager of AI Products and Productivity Tools at IBM. Before joining IBM, he served as Vice President of Data & AI at Microsoft, where he led several data and cloud productivity initiatives and played a key role in integrating AI into these tools. He is the creator of Bob, IBM’s AI software development partner. He pioneered the concepts of “Code as Data” and “Teaching Machines to Code,” land created Microsoft’s first AI code assistant, Intellicode which evolved into GitHub Copilot.He was instrumental in bringing key AI techniques and innovations into the product. Earlier in his career, Neel headed eBay Research and Data Labs, driving advancements in search, recommender systems, computer vision, economics, human-computer interaction, and distributed computing. He began his career at IBM’s Almaden Research Center, where he developed IBM’s first search engine and contributed to foundational work in web mining and XML systems. A frequent speaker at international conferences, Neel holds over 320 patents and has published more than 150 research papers. He earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Indiana University Bloomington and is an alumnus of the Indian Institute of Technology, Bombay.

### Arvid Ottenberg

*Senior Product Manager IBM Bob at IBM — IBM*

Arvid Ottenberg is a Product Manager at IBM, where he owns the roadmap for IBM Bob, IBM's agentic software development partner. He joined Bob as an architect and engineer on the development team ahead of its product launch, before stepping into the product management seat leading the technical PM team. Across more than eight years at IBM, Arvid has spanned software engineering, architecture, consulting, and product management. Previously as a Senior Architect at IBM Consulting, he led enterprise transformations and digital product engineering delivery programs at scale. He holds a Master's in Information Systems Engineering and Management from the KIT Hector School.

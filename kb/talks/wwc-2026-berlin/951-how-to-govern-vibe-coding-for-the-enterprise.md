## Abstract

Explore the critical role of security and governance in AI development and app building within various teams. As organizations scale their AI initiatives, governance isn't just a safeguard; it's the foundation that makes speed sustainable. This is a thought leadership conversation on why enterprise-grade security must be built at the platform level from the start.

*Head of Platform — Retool*

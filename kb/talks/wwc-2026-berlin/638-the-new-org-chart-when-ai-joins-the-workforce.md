# The New Org Chart: When AI Joins the Workforce

**Avani Prabhakar (Chief People  and AI Enablement Officer — Atlassian), Benjamin Mann (CTO — Delivery Hero), Nils Berger (Founder and CEO — Viewpointsystem), Damiana Casile (Director of People Business Partners — Dow Jones)**

`Track: Talent Strategy` · `Type: Panel/Fireside Chat` · `Stage: Stage 4`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_t4ChjPQ3js) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/638)

## Abstract

When AI starts doing real work, it is not just tasks that change but how teams and organizations are built around them. People and engineering leaders explore what the workplace looks like when humans and AI share the load. A practical conversation about how work, roles and management are being redrawn.

## Speakers

### Avani Prabhakar

*Chief People  and AI Enablement Officer — Atlassian*

Atlassian Chief People and AI Enablement Officer, Avani Prabhakar, leads Atlassian's People and Transformations organisation with a sharp focus on unlocking potential in the AI era – blending purpose, systems thinking, and modern leadership to shape the future of work.

Avani’s focus is on enabling the cultural transformation required alongside the technology shift – to build AI-fluent teams and unlock opportunity. She has been instrumental in pioneering the distributed work models and ways of working that underpin Atlassian’s Team Anywhere approach, and is actively embedding AI up-skilling, scaling, and innovation across the organisation.

With 20+ years across tech, aviation, engineering, professional services and consulting, Avani keeps Atlassian's people-first approach practical and grounded, to build human + AI collaboration and capability in complex, high-growth environments.

### Benjamin Mann

*CTO — Delivery Hero*

Benjamin was appointed Chief Technology Officer (CTO) at Delivery Hero in June 2024. He leads the engineering teams driving the technological expertise, operations, and processes supporting Delivery Hero’s platform applications across all its markets. Prior to joining Delivery Hero in January 2023 as Senior Vice President of Engineering, Benjamin was CTO at foodpanda, one of Asia’s leading food delivery apps. With a career spanning 25 years in the technology industry, Benjamin has been and continues to be at the forefront of innovation across the globe.

### Nils Berger

*Founder and CEO — Viewpointsystem*

Nils Berger is the Founder and CEO of Viewpointsystem, a deep-tech company developing and producing internationally awarded smart glasses with integrated eye tracking. With its “Digital Iris” integration platform, Viewpointsystem enables seamless eye tracking in third-party wearables. By capturing gaze in real time, eye tracking enables more intuitive interaction and a deeper connection between users and digital environments. Nils is an experienced founder and serial entrepreneur with a proven track record in information services, technology consultancy, and the automation industry.

### Damiana Casile

*Director of People Business Partners — Dow Jones*

Damiana Casile is the Director of People Business Partners for Europe at Dow Jones. Based in Barcelona, she drives the people strategy and organizational design behind fast-evolving, tech-driven teams. Damiana specializes in scaling inclusive, high-performing cultures and leading change management to turn business transformation into high performance.

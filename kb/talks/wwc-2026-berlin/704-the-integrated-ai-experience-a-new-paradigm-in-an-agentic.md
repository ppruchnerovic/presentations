# The integrated AI experience: A New Paradigm in an Agentic World

**Loredana Crisan (Chief Design Officer — Figma), Markus Rautert (CTO — adidas), Stefan Ostwald (Co-founder — Parloa), Anna Heim (Freelance Reporter — TechCrunch)**

`Track: Strategy & Innovation` · `Type: Panel/Fireside Chat` · `Stage: Mainstage`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SPwDreFnmyA) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/704)

## Abstract

When agents start acting on people's behalf, the customer is no longer the only one you are designing systems for. This panel asks what changes when products are built for agents as much as for humans, and what that means for brands and experience. A look at how the basics of commerce and product shift in an agentic world.

## Speakers

### Loredana Crisan

*Chief Design Officer — Figma*

Loredana Crisan is the Chief Design Officer at Figma, where she leads design, UX writing, brand studio and research. Prior to joining Figma, she spent nine years at Meta, where she held product and design leadership roles across Messenger, Instagram and GenAI initiatives. Before Loredana’s career in product and design, she was a musician and sound engineer.

### Markus Rautert

*CTO — adidas*

Markus Rautert is Chief Technology Officer at adidas, where he leads global technology strategy across enterprise architecture, platform engineering, IT infrastructure, cybersecurity, and Data & AI. With nearly two decades at adidas, Markus has helped build the company’s digital backbone—scaling platforms, securing operations, and enabling data-driven decisions across brand, supply chain, and finance. His current focus is on strengthening cybersecurity and unlocking business value through advanced analytics. Markus speaks regularly at internal and industry events, connecting with developers and senior leaders alike. Offstage, he’s a lifelong gamer and passionate tech enthusiast.

### Stefan Ostwald

*Co-founder — Parloa*

Stefan Ostwald is Chief AI Officer and Co-Founder of Parloa, a leading AI company redefining customer service through AI agents. Stefan's passion for conversational AI dates back to 2016, when he co-founded Future of Voice — one of Europe's first voice AI agencies. In 2018, he went on to found Parloa, where he now leads the agentic AI research team, bridging state-of-the-art research with enterprise-grade solutions. His mission: making AI so reliable and intuitive that every conversation deepens the customer relationship.

### Anna Heim

*Freelance Reporter — TechCrunch*

Anna Heim is a freelance reporter at TechCrunch and event moderator focusing on tech and startups, with a keen eye for innovation outside of Silicon Valley. Her byline has appeared in publications including CNet, Fortune, Resilience Media and The Next Web. A former startup founder and Sciences Po Paris alum, she is fluent in multiple languages, including French, English, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese.

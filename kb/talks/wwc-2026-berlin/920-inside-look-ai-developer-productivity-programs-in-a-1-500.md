# Inside Look: AI & Developer Productivity Programs in a 1,500+ Engineering Team

**Ellen Jones (Customer Success Leader — DX), Kristjan Ulst (Developer Productivity — Arrive)**

## Abstract

As AI transforms software development, engineering leaders are facing new questions: How should developer productivity teams support AI adoption? What standards and guardrails are needed? And how do you measure impact beyond simple usage metrics?

In this fireside-style conversation, Ellen Jones, Customer Success Leader at DX, sits down with Kristjan Ulst, Engineering Manager at Arrive, to explore how their developer productivity program operates and evolves in the age of AI. Drawing on lessons from a 1,500+ engineer environment, we’ll discuss how the team partners across engineering, approaches standardization and enablement, evaluates emerging AI tools, and thinks about measuring outcomes.

The discussion will cover practical considerations around platform engineering, developer experience, AI adoption, productivity measurement, and organizational alignment—along with lessons learned, challenges encountered, and what may be next for developer productivity teams.

Whether you’re building a developer productivity function, leading platform engineering initiatives, or navigating AI adoption at scale, you’ll leave with insights from the front lines of a modern engineering organization.

## Speakers

### Ellen Jones

*Customer Success Leader — DX*

Ellen Abarr (Jones) is a Customer Success Manager at DX, Atlassian's developer intelligence platform, where she partners with enterprise engineering organizations to measure, benchmark, and improve developer productivity. Based in Tennessee, Ellen works directly with engineering leaders to identify workflow friction, quantify the impact of AI investments, and build data-driven strategies that improve developer experience at scale. She brings a practitioner's perspective on what it actually takes to shift engineering culture through measurement.

### Kristjan Ulst

*Developer Productivity — Arrive*

Kristjan Ulst leads Developer Productivity and AI adoption at Arrive, where his team supports 800+ engineers across 90+ countries. He builds the platforms, tooling, and AI workflows that help teams ship faster, safer, and with less friction.

His career sits at the intersection of AI and engineering productivity. At MindTitan, he led teams that shipped AI products for telecom, government, and education. At Arrive, he applies that experience at scale, driving AI coding tool adoption across the engineering org, from strategy and enablement to guardrails and measurement.

Before that, he co-founded two startups (Fanvestory, acquired, and Fairmus) and ran a CTO-as-a-service practice for over a decade. He has built products from zero to one and scaled engineering cultures from startup to enterprise.

What he cares about: making engineers' lives easier, building teams with real ownership and autonomy, and treating AI adoption as a culture change, not a tool rollout.

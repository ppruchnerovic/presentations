## Abstract

AI is becoming a commodity. Every organization can access similar technologies, tools, and models. The real differentiator will not be AI itself, but how quickly an organization can adapt to change. As technology evolves faster than ever, competitive advantage shifts from technology adoption to organizational adaptability.
The organizations that win won't necessarily be those with the best AI strategy. They will be those that learn, adapt, and evolve faster than their competitors.

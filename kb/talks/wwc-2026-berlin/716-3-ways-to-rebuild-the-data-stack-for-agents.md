# 3 Ways to Rebuild the Data Stack for Agents

**Jordan Tigani (CEO — MotherDuck)**

`Track: Data & Databases` · `Type: Keynote/Talk` · `Stage: Stage 12`

`#AI Coding Assistants` `#Analytics` `#Data` `#Databases` `#DuckDB` `#Generative AI (GenAI)`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XlBwD_pBDp8) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/716)

## Abstract

AI agents are becoming core users of the data tools we've used for decades. Seemingly everyone has a dozen agents querying their data warehouse, building dashboards, or writing their data pipelines.

This session will share what we've learned at MotherDuck while building data products for users that rely on AI agents. We'll cover:

- Tradeoffs of owning the agent harness vs. the BYO agent model
- The role of CLIs and Model Context Protocol in the data stack
- How to create products agents can discover, sign up for, and use

## Speakers

### Jordan Tigani

*CEO — MotherDuck*

Jordan is the CEO and Chief Duck Herder of MotherDuck, a data warehouse built on DuckDB. He was previously a founding engineer of Google BigQuery, Director of Product at Google, and Chief Product Officer at SingleStore.

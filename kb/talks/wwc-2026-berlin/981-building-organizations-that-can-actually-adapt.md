# Building Organizations That Can Actually Adapt

**Anastasija Removic (Senior Talent Manager — Liberty Global), Claudia Tattanelli (Founder and CEO — C-YourCulture), Kerstin Wagner (EVP Talent Acquisition — Deutsche Bahn), Nazim Ünlü (Strategic Advisor — allygatr)**

`Track: People & Culture` · `Type: Panel/Fireside Chat` · `Stage: Stage 4`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VI9gbponHdA) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/981)

## Abstract

In a world defined by constant disruption, from AI-driven transformation and shifting workforce expectations to economic uncertainty and rapid technological change, organizational adaptability has become a critical competitive advantage. Yet many companies still struggle to move beyond agility as a buzzword and build the structures, cultures, and leadership practices that enable real, sustained adaptation.

Join Claudia Tatanelli as she moderates an engaging discussion with Kerstin Wagner, Anastasia Removic, and Nazim Ünlü, three leaders at the forefront of organizational transformation. Together, they will explore what it takes to create organizations that can continuously evolve, learn, and thrive in times of change.

The panel will examine how leaders can foster resilience, empower teams to make decisions, balance stability with innovation, and build cultures that embrace experimentation without losing focus. Attendees will gain practical insights into the strategies, mindsets, and operating models that help organizations respond effectively to today's challenges while preparing for tomorrow's opportunities.

*Founder and CEO — C-YourCulture*

Claudia has also been a guest on Bloomberg, Fox, and CNBC’s Morning Call, as well as many other media outlets.

### Kerstin Wagner

*EVP Talent Acquisition — Deutsche Bahn*

At Deutsche Bahn AG, Kerstin Wagner serves as Executive Vice President of Talent Acquisition, reporting directly to the Chief Human Resources Officer. In this role, she consolidates recruiting, employer branding, global governance, and temporary employment under one roof. Her primary objective is to position DB as a top employer and attract talent to the DB Group.

Working with her team, she oversees the recruitment of a workforce equivalent to the size of a small town every year, while ensuring the talent acquisition process remains innovative and modern. Kerstin Wagner is a member of several supervisory boards and chairs the Advisory Board of the Deutsche Bahn Foundation. Additionally, she is a dedicated advocate for New Work, technology, and diversity.

In 2021, she was named one of the "100 Women of the Year" by Focus magazine, and in 2024, Personalmagazin recognized her as one of the 50 most influential HR thought leaders.

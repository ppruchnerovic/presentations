# Building with IBM Bob

**Maximilian Jesch (Outbound Product Manager — IBM Bob)**

`Track: Developer Experience` · `Type: Keynote/Talk` · `Stage: Stage 5`

`#AI Coding Assistants`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=knCF9uZThns) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/928)

## Abstract

A practical, demo-driven introduction to Bob, IBM's AI coding assistant.
We'll start with the essentials—getting set up and working with Bob day to day—then move into the best practices and some of the things that make Bob unique.
You'll see Bob build live, and get a first look at who Bob can help you modernize legacy code in Java, RPG, and COBOL. Come see what you can build with Bob.

## Speakers

### Maximilian Jesch

*Outbound Product Manager — IBM Bob*

Maximilian Jesch is an AI Engineer and Developer Advocate at IBM, where he focuses on Agentic AI and the watsonx ecosystem. In his role, he helps developers and enterprises understand, build, and scale AI solutions, while also serving as a technical leader for IBM Bob. Beyond his work at IBM, Max is active in the broader AI community, sharing insights through open-source projects. His work reflects a blend of hands-on engineering, advocacy, and community leadership in modern AI development.

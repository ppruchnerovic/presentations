## Abstract

The world of search is changing and AI searches (+LLMs) are helping businesses and people to find new products and services. ChatGPT, Claude and others are answering questions about which products to purchase.
Businesses have to start monitoring their visibility in AI search and find new ways to optimize how they are shown against competitors. We are sharing our experiments and learnings in this market - what works, what failed and what we anticipate will happen in the future.

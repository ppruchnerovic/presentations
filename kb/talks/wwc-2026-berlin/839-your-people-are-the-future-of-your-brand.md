# Your People Are The Future Of Your Brand

**Ash Jones (Founder — Great Influence)**

`Track: People & Culture` · `Type: Keynote/Talk` · `Stage: Stage 4`

`#Advocacy` `#Marketing` `#People & Culture`

## Abstract

Trust in brand is declining, with 71% of people saying they trust them less than they did a year ago. In fact, while 90% of executives believe consumers trust their companies, only 30% of consumers actually do.

So, who do people trust? People.

It has never been more evident that your people are the future of your brand. This session explores how brands can leverage the rise of personal branding to promote the people working within them to drive brand and business growth.

## Speakers

### Ash Jones

*Founder — Great Influence*

Ash Jones is the Founder of Great Influence, where they act as a Strategic Partner to global brands helping them activate their senior leadership teams on social media to support external comms, employer brand and company culture.

Ash holds a decade of experience in helping CEOs and Entrepreneurs build their personal brands including Steven Bartlett of Diary Of A CEO fame, ex-Manchester United footballer Gary Neville, best-selling authors Matthew Syed and John Amaechi and senior leaders at the likes of Booking.com and Santander.

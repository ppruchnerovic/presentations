# Software That Fixes Itself

**Milin Desai (CEO — Sentry), Rodrigue Schäfer (VP of Platform — Delivery Hero)**

`Track: Quality & Reliability` · `Type: Keynote/Talk` · `Stage: Mainstage`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=obCGK-uRc6M) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/993)

## Abstract

What if your software could detect, diagnose, and someday even resolve its own issues — before a developer ever gets paged? In this fireside chat, Sentry CEO Milin Desai sits down with Rodrigue Schäfer, Vice President of Platform at Delivery Hero, to explore what "self-healing software" looks like in practice at one of the world's largest creative technology companies. The conversation will cover what self-healing means at Adobe's scale, how it reshapes the day-to-day reality for developers, and perhaps most importantly what engineers are able to focus on when software handles the routine for them. Part technical, part philosophical, this is a candid look at where software reliability is headed and what it means for the humans who build it.

## Speakers

### Milin Desai

*CEO — Sentry*

Milin Desai currently serves as the CEO of Sentry. Used by more than 4 million developers and 150,000 organizations worldwide, Sentry helps every developer detect, understand, and fix broken code, fast. Milin joined Sentry from VMware where he helped build out multiple lines of business from networking (0->1b+) to multi cloud (0->100M+ SAAS). Earlier in his career, he held engineering and product roles at Veritas and Riverbed.

### Rodrigue Schäfer

*VP of Platform — Delivery Hero*

With over two decades of experience at the forefront of the European tech scene in startup and scale-up environments, Rodrigue Schäfer focuses on driving innovation within Delivery Hero, one of the world’s leading local delivery platforms, by providing engineers with the tools and infrastructure they need to deliver high-quality products fast.

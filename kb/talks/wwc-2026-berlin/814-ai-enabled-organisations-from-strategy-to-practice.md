# AI-Enabled Organisations: From Strategy to Practice

**Benedikt Höck (Head of AI — KPMG), Chris Daden (CTO and Head of AI — Criteria), Lars Rogge (COO — Generali Deutschland), Damiana Casile (Director of People Business Partners — Dow Jones)**

`Track: Strategy & Innovation` · `Type: Panel/Fireside Chat` · `Stage: Tech Leaders Stage`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mSc6YJkfcwI) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/814)

## Abstract

Plenty of organizations have an AI strategy on paper; far fewer have it genuinely working. This panel is about closing that gap, with leaders who have moved from ambition to results. A practical conversation about making AI real across a business.

## Speakers

### Benedikt Höck

*Head of AI — KPMG*

Benedikt Höck is Head of Artificial Intelligence (AI) for KPMG in Germany and the EMA region. In this role, he is responsible for the AI go-to-market strategy, the development of service offerings, and the firm’s internal transformation through AI. He supports clients end-to-end on their AI journey - from strategy design and the implementation of high-impact use cases to organizational enablement and ensuring the secure, trusted deployment of AI.

### Chris Daden

*CTO and Head of AI — Criteria*

Chris Daden is the Chief Technology Officer at Criteria, a global Talent Success platform operating at the
center of the Future of Work. Criteria helps organizations measure real talent signals through more than
80 million assessments delivered worldwide in over 25 languages, enabling fairer, more predictive hiring
and development decisions. Backed by over $65 million in private growth equity investment, Criteria
works with 4,000+ leading global organizations to move beyond static credentials toward dynamic,
skills-based insights that reflect true capability and potential.

Chris is a multi-time founder and technology executive with deep expertise in artificial intelligence,
including applied AI systems, model fine-tuning, and frontier model development. His work focuses on
how AI is reshaping work, education, and human capital strategy at scale. He regularly advises leaders
on the intersection of technology, workforce transformation, and organizational design, and frequently
speaks globally as a keynote presenter, podcast guest, and industry contributor.
He is a contributor to the Forbes Technology Council and Forbes Young Executives Council, and serves
as the founding technology advisor for The Hungry Lab, a global initiative working alongside the United
Nations to advise and support startups worldwide. Chris also leads SoCal Tech Forum, one of Southern
California’s largest technology non-profits, dedicated to educating companies and communities on
emerging and disruptive technologies.

Chris brings a data-driven perspective on Work 4.0, the future of education, and how organizations can
design talent systems that prioritize adaptability, equity, and long-term human potential in an AI-driven
world.

### Lars Rogge

*COO — Generali Deutschland*

Lars Rogge has been a member of the Executive Board of Generali Deutschland AG as Chief Operating Officer (COO) since October 1, 2025. In this role, he is responsible for operational management, the further development of IT and business processes, and key transformation initiatives—with a particular focus on digitalization and the use of artificial intelligence.

Rogge brings more than 20 years of international management experience in the insurance and financial services sector. Before joining Generali Deutschland, he served as COO and a member of the Executive Board at Allianz Partners in Paris, where he was globally responsible for operations, claims and all respective transformation programs and advanced the modernization of IT and process landscapes.

Previously, as Global Head of Operations Travel, Assistance & Digital, he led large international units with several thousand employees and consistently drove digital transformation and efficiency improvements. Other stages of his career include senior roles at Allianz Deutschland AG and Allianz SE, as well as at Deutsche Börse AG and the strategy consultancy Roland Berger, where he gained extensive experience in strategy, transformation, and process optimization in a global environment.

Lars Rogge studied business administration at Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich and the University of Passau, graduating with a degree in business administration.

As COO of Generali Deutschland AG, he is particularly focused on driving the integration of innovative technologies into day-to-day operations. He emphasizes the targeted use of AI to boost customer focus, efficiency and innovation and aiming to provide the best possible support to employees while creating sustainable added value for customers.

### Damiana Casile

*Director of People Business Partners — Dow Jones*

Damiana Casile is the Director of People Business Partners for Europe at Dow Jones. Based in Barcelona, she drives the people strategy and organizational design behind fast-evolving, tech-driven teams. Damiana specializes in scaling inclusive, high-performing cultures and leading change management to turn business transformation into high performance.

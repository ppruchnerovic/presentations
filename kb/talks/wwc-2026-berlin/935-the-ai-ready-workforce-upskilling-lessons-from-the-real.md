# The AI-Ready Workforce: Upskilling Lessons from the Real World

**Bernd Datler (Managing Director — ASFINAG), Laura Thorson (SVP Product — GitHub), Mark Pollok (CEO — Trenkwalder Group AG), Hendrikje Rudnick (Managing Editor — BUSINESS INSIDER GERMANY)**

`Track: Strategy & Innovation` · `Type: Panel/Fireside Chat` · `Stage: Tech Leaders Stage`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dpMZq0wW7TQ) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/935)

## Abstract

Everyone agrees the workforce needs to be ready for AI; far fewer have actually reskilled people at scale. This panel brings together very different organizations to share what genuinely works and what does not. Real lessons from the field, not slideware.

## Speakers

### Bernd Datler

*Managing Director — ASFINAG*

Bernd Datler is managing director of the tolling company of ASFINAG since November 2009 and CIO/CDO of the ASFINAG group. Mr. Datler is responsible for technical development and operation of the tolling systems of ASFINAG covering all highways and expressways of Austria as well as for various ITS activities within ASFINAG. In addition to that, Mr. Datler is responsible for all IT- and digitalisation related activities of ASFINAG.

### Laura Thorson

*SVP Product — GitHub*

Luis is the SVP of the Copilot Agents Product, focused on making GitHub the place where humans and agents collaborate to develop software. With a career spanning leadership roles at Microsoft, Reddit, Rappi and Loft, he has a proven track record of scaling high-growth engineering, product, and data organizations. A lifelong builder, Luis started programming when he was eight years old and today also serves on the Board of Trustees for the Wikimedia Foundation.

### Mark Pollok

*CEO — Trenkwalder Group AG*

As CEO of Trenkwalder Group AG, Mark had the opportunity to lead the development of an innovative solution for automating recruitment. His focus was on leveraging technology to optimize and enhance the recruitment process, ensure to remain industry leaders and offer the best possible service to their clients. In addition, Mark and his team successfully built an outsourced customer service solution in which transactions are handled by AI agents. With revenues of €550 million and 16,000 employees across 15 countries, they are proud to deliver first-class services to their clients worldwide.

### Hendrikje Rudnick

*Managing Editor — BUSINESS INSIDER GERMANY*

Hendrikje Rudnick is a business journalist and Managing Editor at Business Insider Germany. She reports on the labor market, careers, and artificial intelligence, with a focus on how these trends are transforming the workplace.

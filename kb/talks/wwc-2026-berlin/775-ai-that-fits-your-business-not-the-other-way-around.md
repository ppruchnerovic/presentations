# AI That Fits Your Business, Not the Other Way Around

**Anshul Jindal (Senior Solution Architect — NVIDIA), Cansu Kavili Örnek (Principal Architect — Red Hat)**

## Abstract

our models. Your data. Your choice.
Most AI platforms expect you to reshape your operations around their constraints: centralise your data, commit to their cloud, accept their model choices. But enterprises don't work that way.
This session challenges the one-size-fits-all approach to enterprise AI. We'll explore why the gap between general AI and your AI is where real value gets created, and examine a different approach: one where models connect to your data wherever it lives, where you choose right-sized models that balance cost and capability, and where you deploy on infrastructure that makes sense for your business. If you're tired of being told to adapt to AI rather than having AI adapt to you, this talk is for you.
Red Hat and NVIDIA will deliver this speech jointly!

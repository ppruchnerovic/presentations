# What 500+ Production Environments Taught Us About Shipping AI Agents

**Liran Hason (VP of AI — Coralogix)**

`Track: AI Agents` · `Type: Keynote/Talk` · `Stage: Stage 2`

`#Agents` `#Agentic AI` `#Multi-Agent Systems` `#Observability`

## Abstract

Building an impressive AI agent demo has never been easier. Shipping it to hundreds of production environments is a completely different problem.
This talk draws on the experience of building and deploying Olly, Coralogix's agentic observability system, across hundreds of organizations. If you're building AI agents - or planning to - you'll leave with a clearer picture of where the real challenges live, and where to focus your energy before your users find the gaps for you.

## Speakers

### Liran Hason

*VP of AI — Coralogix*

Liran Hason is VP of AI at Coralogix, where he leads the company’s AI strategy, architecture and execution across autonomous agents and enterprise AI. A builder, founder, and engineering leader with more than 20 years of experience, he has spent much of his career making AI systems more reliable, responsible, and ready for the real world. Before Coralogix, he founded and served as CEO of Aporia, a pioneer in AI observability and guardrails, later acquired by Coralogix, and earlier was an AI Architect at Adallom, acquired by Microsoft.

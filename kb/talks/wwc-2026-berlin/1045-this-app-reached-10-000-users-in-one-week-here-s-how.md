# This App Reached 10,000 Users in One Week. Here's How.

**Mahran Meißner (Head of Technology and Innovation Space — Siemens)**

`Track: AI Engineering` · `Type: Keynote/Talk` · `Stage: Stage 1`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ulTTDSGuAYI) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/1045)

## Abstract

One week before launch, this application didn't exist. Seven days later it was live, running in production, secured with enterprise authentication, deployed to the enterprise infrastructure, and being used by more than 10,000 employees world wide.

This session is about what happens when AI goes beyond helping developers write code—and starts acting as an entire software engineering team.

Using a real World Cup application built at Siemens as the case study, I'll walk through the complete journey from a simple business idea to a production-ready application. We'll look beyond code generation and explore how AI can drive discovery, requirements, architecture, implementation, testing, infrastructure, and deployment—while humans stay in control of the decisions that matter.

I'll also share the biggest lessons from recent visits to leading Silicon Valley companies and explain why the next generation of software engineering isn't about better prompts or faster coding. It's about orchestrating teams and AI on top of enterprise developer platforms.

If you've ever wondered what software engineering might look like in five years, this session will show you a production system - CREATE - at Siemens.

## Speakers

### Mahran Meißner

*Head of Technology and Innovation Space — Siemens*

Mahran Meißner is Head of the Technology & Innovation Space at Siemens IT, where he focuses on turning emerging technologies into business value. His work spans AI agents, software engineering, humanoid robots, XR, and digital twins, with a strong emphasis on making advanced technologies accessible across the enterprise. He is the creator of CREATE, an AI platform that enables anyone to transform ideas into production-ready software. Mahran is a frequent speaker on enterprise AI, innovation, and the future of software development, sharing practical insights from building AI-powered products inside a global organization.

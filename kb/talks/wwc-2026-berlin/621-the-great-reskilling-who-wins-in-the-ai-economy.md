# The Great Reskilling - Who Wins in the AI Economy?

**Daniel Terzenbach (Member of the  Executive Board — Federal Employment Agency), Johannes Kopf (CEO — Public Employment Service Austria (AMS)), Rudi Bauer (CCO — WeAreDevelopers)**

`Track: People & Culture` · `Type: Panel/Fireside Chat` · `Stage: Stage 4`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KDb-WcZwaHo) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/621)

## Abstract

For years, the conversation about the future of work was dominated by one question: Will technology replace jobs? Today, a more important question is emerging: How do we ensure people can continuously reinvent themselves in a world where jobs, skills, and industries change faster than ever?
Europe faces a unique challenge: AI is accelerating transformation, demographics are shrinking the workforce, skill shortages are growing, and millions of workers may need to reskill throughout their careers. To discuss how we can turn disruption into opportunity, we are delighted to welcome two of Europe's leading voices on labor market transformation... Johannes Kopf and Daniel Terzenbach

## Speakers

### Daniel Terzenbach

*Member of the  Executive Board — Federal Employment Agency*

Daniel Terzenbach has been a Member of the Executive Board of the Federal Employment Agency since 2019, where he is responsible for operational business areas.

From October 2023 to July 2024, he also served as the Federal Government’s Special Representative for the labor market integration of refugees.

At the beginning of his career with the Federal Employment Agency, he held a leadership position at the Jobcenter Märkischer Kreis. In 2009, he moved to the agency’s headquarters in Nuremberg, where he took on various leadership roles and, from 2015 to 2018, was responsible for the labor market integration of refugees.

### Johannes Kopf

*CEO — Public Employment Service Austria (AMS)*

Johannes Kopf, LL.M. is the Chairman of the Executive Board of the Public Employment Service Austria, AMS, where he continues to shape Austria's policymaking for labor market and employment.

Prior to becoming executive chairman in July 2023, Johannes served as a member of the Executive Board of the Public Employment Service Austria from July 2006 to June 2023, as well as Chairman of the Network of European Public Employment Services (PES) from July 2019 to June 2023.

Born and raised in Vienna, Austria, Johannes holds a doctorate in law and an LL.M. in European law. He is a well-respected labor market expert, having gained valuable professional experience in the cabinet of former Minister for Economics and Labour, Dr. Martin Bartenstein, and as a member of the Board of Directors of the AMS Austria, both from 2003 to mid-2006.

Before his time in the ministry, he was a consultant for the Federation of Austrian Industries, also with a focus on labour market and employment policies.

In addition to his ongoing work at AMS Austria, Johannes was awarded the Humanity Prize of the Heinrich Treichl Foundation by the Austrian Red Cross in 2017. He is also an award-winning photographer and highly regarded DJ.

# You Can't Automate Trust — The Future of Recruiting is Still Human

**Mahmoud Aly (TA Business Partner — Semrush)**

`Track: People & Culture` · `Type: Keynote/Talk` · `Stage: Stage 4`

`#Talent & Recruiting`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rWKl7SSoMdY) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/592)

## Abstract

AI is transforming talent acquisition at a speed no one predicted. And yet, candidate experience is declining, and recruiters' productivity is not 'supercharged' yet. Somewhere in the race to automate everything, we forgot about the person on the other end of the process, and we forgot about what talent leaders' true edge is.

In this keynote, Mahmoud will make the case for the hardest thing in hiring, and the most overlooked. Trust takes time, intention, and a human being willing to show up. It can't be generated, scaled, or prompted. And in an era where everything else can be automated, it's the only thing that actually wins.

## Speakers

### Mahmoud Aly

*TA Business Partner — Semrush*

Mahmoud is a Talent Acquisition expert with over a decade of experience recruiting for some of the world's most recognizable brands, including Nike, Semrush, Samsung, and PepsiCo.

Throughout his career, Mahmoud has taken on several Talent Acquisition roles, from Tech Recruiting, Talent Sourcing, Executive hiring, to Talent Ops.
He currently leads global Go-To-Market Recruitment at Semrush, and previously led the Data & AI business at Nike.
He's also the Founder of Egypt Recruiting Community, the first and largest network in Egypt for the People & Culture space.

Mahmoud is an international keynote speaker who has delivered keynotes at The Sourcing Summit, HR Leaders Summit, and The Recruitment Conference, among others.

## Abstract

What happens inside the computation itself when Agentic AI is moving faster than most security teams can keep up with? AI agents are executing workflows, calling tools, accessing sensitive data, and making decisions autonomously across infrastructure that organizations don't fully own or control. Learn how NVIDIA Confidential Computing protects data in use to assure data integrity in the era of agentic AI.

## Speakers

### Rob Nertney

*Principal Software Architect — NVIDIA*

Rob Nertney is a principal software architect for confidential computing. He has spent nearly 15 years architecting the features and deployment of accelerator hardware into hyperscale environments for both internal and external use by developers. He has several patents in processor design relating to secure solutions that are in production today.

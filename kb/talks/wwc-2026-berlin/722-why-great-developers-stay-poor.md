## Abstract

Most developers are excellent at what they do. They deliver clean code, meet deadlines, and keep clients happy. And yet, many of them whether freelancing or running their own IT business  struggle to build financial stability, charge what they're worth, or land clients who actually value their work.
Jawed Sahebjan spent years in that exact position  executing projects and waiting for the next contract. Until he changed one thing of how he thought about his role.
In this talk, he shares how he went from short-term contractor to strategic partner generating over 1 million euros in revenue with just a handful of clients. No team. Just a fundamental shift in how he positioned himself.
You will leave with a concrete understanding of why technical excellence alone keeps you stuck, and what actually moves the needle  whether you are an IT freelancer or running your own tech business.

## Speakers

### Jawed Sahebjan

*Software Engineer and Technical Lead — IT-Freelancer Forge*

Ahmad Jawed Sahebjan is an IT professional with over a decade of experience as a lead developer and senior IT consultant, having worked with organizations such as Volkswagen, Deutsche Bank, Postbank, R+V, and Deutsche Telekom.
Over the years he has worked alongside numerous IT freelancers, giving him a clear picture of what separates those who thrive from those who stay stuck competing on hourly rates. This experience led him to found IT-Freelance-Forge, a platform helping IT freelancers and entrepreneurs in the DACH region build strategic client relationships and financial independence.
He regularly shares insights on his YouTube channel: youtube.com/@JawedSahebjan

# AI in High-Stakes Industries: Lessons Learned

**Alexandre Guenoun (CEO and Founder — Kiro), Nicolò Robba (Chief Product Officer — Prima), Tomislav Tipurić (CTO — Nephos)**

`Track: Strategy & Innovation` · `Type: Panel/Fireside Chat` · `Stage: Tech Leaders Stage`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ClmXysw8N2c) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/903)

## Abstract

In fields like healthcare and insurance, a wrong AI output is not a minor bug - it carries real consequences. The people on this stage build and run AI where mistakes are expensive, and they come together to talk about what trustworthy AI really demands. Hard-won lessons from the high-stakes end of the spectrum.

## Speakers

### Alexandre Guenoun

*CEO and Founder — Kiro*

Alexandre is the founder and CEO of Kiro. He defines Kiro’s vision and strategic direction and serves as the guardian of the company’s culture.

He began his career in private equity at L Catterton (LVMH) and in management consulting at Oliver Wyman. Alexandre graduated from ESSEC in Paris (Management) and from UC Berkeley in California (Innovation and Law), and specialized in innovation at the Luigi Bocconi University in Milan. He was recently named among Forbes’ 30 Under 30 and Choiseul’s 100. Passionate about the contribution of new technologies to improving healthcare at scale, Alexandre decided to create a leading company to help every patient benefit from technology and access better care.

### Nicolò Robba

*Chief Product Officer — Prima*

Nicolò brings international experience in product strategy and leadership to Prima. He began his career at Rocket Internet before moving to Lazada and later Alibaba, where he helped shape product strategy across six Southeast Asian markets. In 2018, he co-founded Lifepal, building it into Indonesia’s leading digital insurance marketplace with over one million policies issued. He then led digital transformation at Oona Insurance and served as SVP Product at Shipper, driving major efficiency gains while sustaining rapid growth. Nicolò holds a Master’s degree from ESADE Business School.

### Tomislav Tipurić

*CTO — Nephos*

Tomislav Tipurić is a business and technology professional who connects technical expertise with business strategy to clarify new technologies and drive corporate innovation. As a Chief Technology Officer and Lead Hackathon Coach, he focuses on artificial intelligence, multi-cloud platforms, and modern software development practices. He has designed more than 20 custom learning hackathons and delivered over 500 hands-on educational experiences, including more than 150 sessions dedicated to machine learning, generative AI, and agentic AI solutions. Tomislav translates technical concepts into business strategies, delivering workshops and lectures to C-level executives on the business impact of AI in their industries. Additionally, he has experience in sales enablement and corporate training, serving as a program trainer to help enterprise partners market, sell, and adopt generative AI and cloud productivity tools, and delivering cloud sales enablement programs for global telecommunications firms. Tomislav is certified and authorized as an official trainer by Google and Microsoft.

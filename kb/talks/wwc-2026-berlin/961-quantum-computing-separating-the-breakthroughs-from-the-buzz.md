Dr. Marcus Hennig serves as Chief Business Officer at planqc, where he is responsible for the strategic development and implementation of quantum solutions. In this role, he operates at the intersection of high-performance computing, technology, and business innovation.

A physicist with a background in mathematical finance, he contributed to international research projects in France, China, and Australia before spending more than a decade in consulting, supporting companies in the development and implementation of new business models.

With his experience in building innovation units and his deep expertise in quantum computing and emerging technologies, Hennig combines scientific excellence with strong business execution capabilities. His mission is to help companies strategically leverage quantum computing to drive growth, efficiency, and sustainable competitive advantage.

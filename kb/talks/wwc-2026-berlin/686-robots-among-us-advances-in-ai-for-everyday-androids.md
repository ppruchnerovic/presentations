# Robots Among us: Advances in AI for Everyday Androids

**Florian Gather (Associate Partner — TNG), Thomas Endres (Managing Partner — TNG)**

`#AI Models` `#Model Training` `#Reinforcement Learning` `#Robotics`

## Abstract

Robotics is transitioning from factory floors to our everyday lives, particularly through humanoid robots. This session features a live demonstration of our Unitree G1 android, exploring how advances in AI using Robotic Foundational Models, increasing data availability, and advanced hardware are transforming science fiction into reality.

The session begins with an overview of robotics. Next, robot training is examined, demonstrating how integrated hardware and software bridge the gap between simulation and real-world deployment. Finally, Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models are explored for enabling meaningful human-robot interaction and future applications.

## Speakers

### Florian Gather

*Associate Partner — TNG*

After earning his PhD in Physics, Dr. Florian Gather transitioned to software development and joined TNG, where he currently works as an Associate Partner. He specializes in analyzing software projects - conducting due diligence, architecture reviews, and technical assessments to help organizations improve system resilience and maintainability. Florian founded ArchUnit.NET, an open-source tool for enforcing architectural rules in .NET projects. With TNG’s Innovation Hacking team, he drives robotics initiatives that explore LLM integration and Robotics Foundational Models (RFMs), alongside prototypes like smart office systems and interactive furniture. Passionate about microprocessor electronics and smart home automation, Florian explores practical ways to merge hardware tinkering with modern software practices.

### Thomas Endres

*Managing Partner — TNG*

Thomas Endres is a Managing Partner for TNG Technology Consulting in Munich. Besides his normal work for the company and the management of the customers he is creating various prototypes, he and the the Innovation Hacking Team develop various prototypes, including an Augmented Reality application that displays the world from an artist's perspective, real-time deepfakes, or an AI for generating presentations. He works on applications in the field of AR/VR, AI, and gesture control to e.g. autonomously fly quadcopters or control them without physical contact. Furthermore, he is involved in various Open Source projects.

Thomas studied computer science at TU Munich and is a passionate software developer. As an Intel Software Innovator and Black Belt, he presents new technologies such as AI, AR/VR, and robotics worldwide. For this, he has received awards including a JavaOne Rockstar Award and several Best Speaker Awards.

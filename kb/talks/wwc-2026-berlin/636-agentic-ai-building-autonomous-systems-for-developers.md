# Agentic AI: building autonomous systems for developers

**Anshul Jindal (Senior Solution Architect — NVIDIA), Sebastian Mahr (Chief Development Architect — SAP)**

## Abstract

The #1 developer topic of 2026. Covers agent architectures, tool-calling, multi-agent orchestration, and real production patterns using NIM microservices and the NVIDIA AgentIQ toolkit. Directly addresses what every developer wants to build next.

## Speakers

### Anshul Jindal

*Senior Solution Architect — NVIDIA*

Anshul is a Sr. Solution Architect at NVIDIA's DGX Cloud team, he specializes in assisting customers with deploying their workloads at scale. Anshul has a strong background in SRE and has extensive experience in managing production-grade Kubernetes clusters across various Cloud Service Providers (CSPs). He has received Ph.D. in computer science from TU Munich, graduating summa cum laude.

### Sebastian Mahr

*Chief Development Architect — SAP*

Sebastian Mahr is an SAP Chief Development Architect with over 15 years of experience at SAP, where he has held various roles spanning enterprise software development and architecture. His deep expertise in SAP's technology ecosystem has positioned him at the forefront of integrating artificial intelligence and machine learning capabilities into enterprise solutions, with a particular emphasis on emerging AI paradigms and their security implications.

Currently, Sebastian's work centers on agentic security—exploring the intersection of autonomous AI agents and cybersecurity. He focuses on understanding how AI agents operate within enterprise environments, addressing the unique security challenges they present, and developing frameworks to ensure safe and responsible deployment of agentic AI systems. His combination of enterprise architecture experience and cutting-edge AI security research makes him a thought leader in preparing organizations for the next generation of intelligent, autonomous systems.

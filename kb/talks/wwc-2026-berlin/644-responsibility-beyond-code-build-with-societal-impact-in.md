## Abstract

In an era of rapid technological acceleration, the responsibility of a builder or product creator extends far beyond deploying the most advanced technology. True innovation requires an unwavering commitment to understanding the broader societal footprint of our systems. This keynote explores how anchoring product development in human-centric principles is not just an ethical imperative, but a strategic advantage that shapes public perception and drives sustainable user adoption.

We will delve into the evolving paradigm of Artificial Intelligence, advocating for a design philosophy centered on collaboration rather than substitution—creating AI that elevates and assists humanity rather than replacing it. Moving from theory to practice, this session will showcase the methodologies and real-world approaches implemented at German Business Directories (Gelbe Seiten, Das Telefonbuch, Das Örtliche). Attendees will discover how prioritizing societal well-being and building trust can seamlessly coexist with—and actively propel—commercial and technical success.

## Speakers

### Yiyang Lu

*Head of R&D — pdm*

Yiyang Lu is a self-taught engineer and entrepreneur at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and robotics. As the founder behind initiatives such as pluto and pdm R&D Labs, he is dedicated to developing advanced technologies that prioritize human-centric design and societal impact.

With a multidisciplinary background spanning from quantitative analysis at the London School of Economics to practical engineering, Yiyang advocates for a future where innovation acts as a catalyst for human capability rather than a replacement. Currently based in Berlin, he is active in the tech ecosystem, focusing on methodologies that bridge the gap between complex technical implementation and ethical, real-world application.

# Physical AI: The Era of Intelligent Machines

**Dr. Ramin Hasani (CEO and Co-founder — Liquid AI), Vinesh Sukumar (VP Product Management and AI/Gen AI — Qualcomm Technologies), Zohar Fox (CEO and Co-founder — Aurora Labs), Clemens Wasner (CEO — enliteAI)**

`Track: Emerging Technologies` · `Type: Panel/Fireside Chat` · `Stage: Tech Leaders Stage`

[Watch the recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TaxjojT2jDU) · [Session page](https://app.wearedevelopers.com/events/16/session/782)

## Abstract

AI is moving off the screen and into the physical world, where the rules are very different. This panel brings together the people building that future to discuss where intelligent machines are real today and where it is still genuinely hard. A grounded look at what it takes for AI to act in the real world.

## Speakers

### Dr. Ramin Hasani

*CEO and Co-founder — Liquid AI*

Dr. Ramin Hasani is the co-founder and CEO of Liquid AI.  Previously, he was jointly appointed as a Principal Machine Learning Scientist at the Vanguard Group and a Research Affiliate at MIT CSAIL.  Ramin’s research focuses on robust deep learning and decision-making in complex dynamical systems.  Prior to that, he was a Postdoctoral Associate at CSAIL MIT, leading research on modeling intelligence and sequential decision-making.  He received his Ph.D. degree with distinction in Computer Science from the Vienna University of Technology, Austria.  His Ph.D. dissertation and continued research on Liquid Neural Networks have been recognized internationally with numerous nominations and awards.

### Vinesh Sukumar

*VP Product Management and AI/Gen AI — Qualcomm Technologies*

Dr. Vinesh Sukumar currently serves as Vice President of Product Management at Qualcomm Technologies, Inc. Vinesh acts as head of AI/ML, leading AI product definition, strategy and solution deployment across multiple business units.

He has about 20 years of industry experience spread across research, engineering and application deployment. He holds a doctorate degree specializing in imaging and vision systems and also holds an MBA focused on strategy and marketing. He is a regular speaker in many AI industry forums and has authored several journal papers and two technical books.

### Zohar Fox

*CEO and Co-founder — Aurora Labs*

Zohar Fox is the Co-Founder and CEO of Aurora Labs, the company behind LOCI - bringing execution reasoning into modern software development.
Awarded the 2021 Artificial Intelligence Excellence Award, Zohar is now leading the transformation of how software is developed, deployed, and maintained, across both automotive and AI factory environments. LOCI by Aurora Labs makes large-scale rollouts more predictable while improving runtime performance and reducing energy consumption in production code. Zohar's vision bridges embedded systems and large-scale inference workloads, delivering observability and optimization directly at the binary and hardware levels, before systems even reach the test bench. Prior to founding Aurora Labs, Zohar spent two decades in embedded hardware/software design, orchestration, and deployment for TELCOs and technology service providers, helping deliver over 10 million connected devices across automotive, banking, and IoT industries. Mr. Fox have also led strategic sales and technology partnerships with Deutsche Telekom, France Telecom, DSP Group, and BOSCH.

### Clemens Wasner

*CEO — enliteAI*

Clemens Wasner is CEO of enliteAI, an Austrian venture studio that achieved its first AI exit just 18 months after investment. He is also founder and chairperson of AI Austria, an independent think tank driving Austria’s competitiveness in artificial intelligence. Since 2017 he has published the Austrian AI Landscape, the reference overview of the country’s AI ecosystem. Clemens hosts the weekly “AI Talk” podcast and is an active member of the European AI Forum, a coalition of national AI associations that advises on and shapes EU regulation.

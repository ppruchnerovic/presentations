## Abstract

Building Intelligent Enterprise Operations with AI, Automation and Decision
Intelligence
Global Capability Centers are evolving into AI-powered innovation hubs, driving the next generation of enterprise operations. This session explores how enterprise architecture, AI, automation, and decision intelligence can be combined to build intelligent command centers that enable faster, more informed decision-making. Designed for developers, architects, and technology leaders, it highlights the key architectural, implementation, and governance considerations for deploying scalable, enterprise-ready AI solutions.

## Speakers

### Sandeep Kusuma

*Enterprise AI Transformation Leader — Bosch Global Software Technologies*

Sandeep Kusuma is an Enterprise AI and Technology Leader at Bosch Global Software Technologies, based in Germany, with more than two decades of experience helping enterprises turn digital transformation into measurable business value. His background spans enterprise technology, Industry 4.0, IoT, AI, and large-scale delivery, with experience shaped by work across European and Indian business ecosystems and global capability center aligned delivery models. Operating at the intersection of strategy, innovation, and execution, he helps organizations build scalable capabilities and convert emerging technologies into tangible business outcomes. He brings deep experience in driving AI-led enterprise transformation across factories, supply chains, connected assets, and field services.
